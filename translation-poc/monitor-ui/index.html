Connect your first translated call

  1. Place a call through Asterisk

    This monitor observes calls. Start the phone call from your SIP client using your configured two-participant dialplan.

  2. Connect both participants

    Send caller and agent channels to /media with the same CALL_ID, distinct ROLE values, and reciprocal SOURCE_LANG / TARGET_LANG.

  3. Wait for “Translating”

    The call appears here automatically. Open it to follow speech, translations, and the health of each direction.

Not seeing a call? Check Asterisk routing and the bridge logs. The bundled 5000 extension is a transport test; paired translation needs the two-participant dialplan described in the project README.
